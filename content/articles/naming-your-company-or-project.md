*Hello, My Name Is Awesome: How to Create Brand Names That Stick* by Alexandra Watkins will get you there. If reading a whole book feels like too much, these two articles should be enough:

- The art and science of brand naming
- Brand naming: how to name a brand in 5 steps

Now that you understand the concept, get to work: use [Namelix](https://namelix.com) to test your creativity and give your business a name.
