Most of what we chase is noise. The next launch, the next country, the next number.

We move fast enough to forget we are already somewhere.

Time is not the scarce thing. It passes at the same rate whatever you do with it. Attention is scarce, and where it goes is what a life turns out to be made of.

Stillness is not the opposite of work. It is the condition for doing any of it well.

Technology is worth building. It is not worth living inside.

The screen is a window, not a wall.
